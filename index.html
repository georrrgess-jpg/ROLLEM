          How to play

          1. Roll the die. 23 letters, A to W. Whatever it lands on is your letter.
          2. Answer the prompt with something that starts with that letter. Type it or tap the mic. The clock is ticking!
          3. Lock it in. Small typos get fixed for you. Check the fix, then tap Submit.
          4. Hit the board. We surveyed 100 people. Match a top-5 answer to score its survey number. The #1 answer pays the most.
          5. Off the board? A valid answer from our answer pool still earns 5. If we don't recognise it, the next player judges it, or in Strict mode it scores nothing.
          6. Bonuses. Hit the board fast for up to +10. Hit it on back-to-back turns for a streak bonus.
          7. Reroll. Hate your letter? Every player gets one reroll per game, but the clock keeps running.
          8. ⚡ Face-Off style. Two players share the phone, one at each end. When the letter lands, the first to buzz answers. Hit #1 and you win the face-off; hit a lower answer and your rival gets one shot to steal with a higher one. A wrong buzz costs 5 points. The face-off winner earns +15.
          9. Final round. All points are doubled (or tripled), so anyone can still win.
